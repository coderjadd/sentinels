The Charter

The conduct expected of every Sentinel.

To carry the title of Sentinel is to accept a standard. The Charter is the line beneath which no member of the Pathway is permitted to stand — regardless of rank, regardless of seniority, regardless of the company they keep.

  1. Maturity in all bearings. The Sentinel does not bait, mock, or escalate. We meet provocation with composure. We do not bring our own weather into the room.
  2. Obedience to the Jedi Code. The Code is the foundation under everything we do. A Sentinel who breaks faith with the Code breaks faith with the Pathway.
  3. Grammar in-game, at all times. Every Sentinel writes in full sentences. No abbreviations of convenience, no chat shorthand, no half-words. The way we speak in uniform reflects the Order.
  4. Respect for rank, in both directions. Senior members do not lord. Junior members do not slacken. Address by title, follow lawful direction, and raise grievances through the chain — not around it.
  5. Attendance kept faithfully. Trainings are the Pathway's working day. Members are expected to attend, host, or co-host as their rank requires. Persistent absence without notice is grounds for review.
  6. Confidentiality of the watch. Internal Pathway business stays internal. Sentinels do not discuss disciplinary matters or member files outside the proper channels.
  7. No conduct unbecoming. Toxicity, harassment, slurs, or behaviour that brings the Pathway into disrepute are immediate grounds for removal.
  8. Honesty before the Pathway. A Sentinel does not lie to High Command. Mistakes are recoverable; deception is not.
Standard The Charter is enforced at every rank. The Overseer is held to the same line as the Pledge.
