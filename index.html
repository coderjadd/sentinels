— What this is —

A division for those who do quiet work.

Progression

Ten ranks across four tiers, from Pledge to Overseer. The work that earns each step is written down.

Mastery

Three disciplines — Combat, Law, and General. Every Sentinel passes them all; none counts alone.

The Sentinel's name is the Sentinel's bond. Composure, grammar, and bearing are the daily standard.
