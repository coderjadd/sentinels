High Command

The senior ranks who steward the Pathway.

Sentinel Masters Held by the Council of Masters

The Pathway's senior watchkeepers. Masters guide instruction, sit on internal review, and represent the Sentinels in matters where the Overseer cannot personally appear. Conferred jointly by the Overseer and Grand Marshal — never solicited.

Grand Marshal The standing Marshal

Second of the Pathway. The Marshal carries the Overseer's authority in absence and holds operational command of the Watch. Where the Overseer sets the line, the Marshal walks it.

Sentinel Overseer RealJadd

The seat of the Pathway. Appointed by the Council of Reconciliation Oversight. Final arbiter of advancement, charter, and the conduct of the Watch.

Note The line is held by those who walk it. High Command is bound to the Charter at the same standard as a Pledge.
