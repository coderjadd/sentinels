Sentinel Honor

The Watch's internal currency. Earned in the work, spent at the ledger.

Sentinel Honor (SH) is the Pathway's recognition of contribution — a quiet ledger of the time a Sentinel has put in. It is not a payment. It is a record, redeemable at the Sentinel Honor shop for goods that mark a member's place in the Watch.

How Sentinel Honor is earned

  1. Server level-upsActive engagement in the Discord + 10 SH per level
  2. Hosting trainings & eventsHosts and co-hosts on record + 10 SH per session
  3. Attending trainings & eventsAttendance recorded by the Host + 10 SH per session
