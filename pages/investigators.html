Jedi Investigators

A specialised division tasked with the investigation and enforcement of regulations within the Jedi Temple(s).

The Investigators are focused on conducting their duties of maintaining peace within Jedi territories — with a focus on justice, fairness, and integrity. Where the standing Watch holds the line, the Investigators are the line behind the line.

Division sealLaw · Moderation · Integrity
"The value of moving slowly is that one can always clearly see the way ahead." — Master Tera Sinube

Rules & Regulations

  • Be Initiate or above
  • Knowledge on Laws and Punishments
  • No recent infractions & a solid reputation
  • Must be in the Sentinel Pathway, or none at all

What we offer

  • Access to Holding Cells & Chamber of Law
  • Ability to enforce laws & keep the Temple safe
  • Access to Jail Tool & moderation commands
  • Fun community & events

Two ways in

— Way I

Tryout

Attend a scheduled Jedi Investigator Tryout when announced. Tryouts are held at advertised times and are open to anyone who meets the rules & regulations above.

See onboarding
— Way II

Application

Submit the Jedi Investigator application form. Applications are reviewed by the standing Instructor and HICOM, and may be followed by an interview.
