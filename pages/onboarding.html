Onboarding

What is asked of those who would seek a place at the line.

The Pathway is open to those who will keep faith with the Charter. The boarding process is straightforward — and it is the only way in.

Begin Onboarding Roblox Group

  1. Join the Discord server.

    Entry to the Pathway begins in the Sentinels' Discord. There is no off-platform petition.

  2. Open #boarding-process.

    Follow the instructions inside, in order, without skipping. Read every line.

  3. Stand the watch as a Pledge.

    On completion, you are a Sentinel Pledge. From there, the Path is the Path — the Handbook Quiz, one of each training, and Acolyte within two weeks.

  4. Join the Roblox group.

    The official in-game home for the Sentinel identity, ranks, and presence. Join it once you have boarded so your in-game tag matches the work.

What we look for Punctuality. Composure under correction. Willingness to read the room. The Pathway can teach forms; it cannot teach bearing.
