The Order

The Sentinel walks the line between what the Code asks and what the galaxy demands.

The Sentinel Pathway is the Order's instrument of the long watch. Where the Consular tends the inner light and the Guardian meets the open blade, the Sentinel is given the work in between — the work of investigation, of instruction, of the patrol that ends without incident and is therefore never written down.

We are Jedi who do not chase the spectacle. Our victories are quiet. A pledge corrected before they fall. A dispute settled before it becomes a duel. A trespass observed and noted and brought, in time, before the Council. The lightsaber is not our first tool. It is rarely our last.

This is patient work. It rewards those who can read a room, hold a line, and keep faith with comrades who may not see them again until the next training.

— The Three Disciplines —
— Discipline I

Combat

The blade as last resort. Form, restraint, and the discipline that ends a fight in one motion or none at all.

— Discipline II

Law

The Code, the Charter, and the precedents of the Order. The Sentinel must know the rule before applying it.

— Discipline III

General

Bearing, conduct, the running of the patrol. The work that is everything else, and most of the work.
