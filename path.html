The Path

Ten ranks. Four tiers. From the boarding gate to the seat of the Overseer.

Tier I · Initiation
I
Sentinel Pledge
The boarding rank — entry held until proven.
  • Complete the Sentinels' boarding-process
Tier II · Low Watch
II
Sentinel Acolyte
First proven step — attend, study, stand.
  • Pass one of each training: Law, Combat, General
  • Pass the Handbook Quiz within two attempts
  • Acolyte must be obtained within two weeks of joining (Temple Guardians exempt)
III
Sentinel Neophyte
Tested past first formation — entrusted with regular watch.
  • Hold Acolyte for a minimum of three days
  • Attend and pass five of each training
Tier III · Knighthood
IV
Jedi Sentinel
Knighthood — the first to bear the title Jedi.
  • Hold Neophyte for three days
  • Pass the Advanced Jedi Knight Trials (Temple Guardians exempt)
  • Hold two pathway endorsements
V
Jedi Diplomat
The Knight who has begun to carry the Order's voice.
  • Hold Jedi Sentinel for one week
  • Co-host one of each training
  • Attend and pass eight of each training
  • Attend two Sentinel Patrols
  • Host three Introductory Trainings as a Jedi Knight
Tier IV · Mastery
VI
Jedi Artisan
Senior Knight — trusted with the shaping of others.
  • Handpicked by the Sentinel Overseer or Sentinel HICOM
  • Or — alternatively — pass the Jedi Artisan Trials
VII
Sentinel Instructor
The hand that teaches — custodian of the trainings.
  • Handpicked by the Sentinel Overseer or Sentinel HICOM
Tier V · High Command
VIII
Sentinel Master
Senior watchkeepers — mastery is conferred, not requested.
  • Receive a handpick from the Sentinel Overseer and Grand Marshal
IX
Grand Marshal
Second of the Pathway — at the Overseer's right hand.
  • Receive a handpick from the Sentinel Overseer
X
Sentinel Overseer
The seat of the Pathway — currently held by RealJadd.
  • Be appointed by the Council of Reconciliation Oversight
Notice Failing the Handbook Quiz twice will result in a purge from the Pathway, with a one-month blacklist.
