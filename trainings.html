The Trainings

Three disciplines. Three sessions. Every Sentinel passes them all.

Trainings are the Pathway's working day. They are pinged through @Training Ping, scheduled by Hosts and overseen by the standing Instructor. Most start "when everyone arrives", are run at the Peace Tree, and last as long as they need to.

Every Sentinel, regardless of seniority, is expected to attend trainings as their rank requires — and to host or co-host once they are eligible. The trainings are not a hurdle to clear; they are the work itself.

— The Three Trainings —
— Discipline I

Combat

"The blade as last resort."

Form, footwork, restraint. Saber engagement, defensive posture, and the discipline of when not to draw.

— Discipline II

Law

"Know the rule before applying it."

The Charter, the Jedi Code, and the operating procedures. Recall, reasoning, and the ability to cite the rule one is enforcing.

— Discipline III

General

"The work that is everything else."

Bearing, communication, conduct of the watch. Patrol procedure, in-game etiquette, escalation. Most of the job lives here.

How a training runs

A Host announces the training in @Training Ping, with the standing Instructor credited as Supervisor. Sentinels gather at the Peace Tree on the linked profile. The training starts when everyone arrives. Attendance and passes are recorded by the Host and confirmed by the Supervisor.

Hosting privileges scale with rank — Pledges and Acolytes attend, Neophytes and above may co-host, and Jedi Diplomats and above are expected to host outright as part of their requirements.

Both attending and hosting trainings yield Sentinel Honor — +10 SH per session, either way. See the Sentinel Honor page.

On grammar All in-game communication during trainings — and at all other times — is held to the Charter's grammar standard.
